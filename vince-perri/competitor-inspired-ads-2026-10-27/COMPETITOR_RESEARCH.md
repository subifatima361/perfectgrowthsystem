## What's working across the niche
1. **One clear idea per image.** There's one metaphor or contrast per ad (paper vs practice, newspaper, chalkboard, split photo).
2. **The founder's real face**, or the founder holding the offer, is in most of the ads that have run the longest.
3. **Callout pill for revenue band / industry** at the top.
4. **Highlight 2–4 words** in one accent color (green/yellow/orange). Vince's version uses yellow #FFDE6A or gold #C39D50.
5. **A pill-shaped CTA inside the image** ("Get the free report →"), placed under the visual.
6. **Familiar formats** (newspaper, chalkboard, Notes app, business card) instead of plain designed graphics.
