# Lacey Newman ads: ChatGPT image prompts (literal version)

Six ads (1A, 1D, 2B, 2E, 3A, 3D) have a hatched **IMAGE SLOT** in the PNG. Each prompt below shows that ad's headline happening in the picture, with exact on-screen text and interface, so the image carries the message on its own.

## How to use

1. **Paste one prompt per chat** into ChatGPT. Pasting all six into one chat makes the images drift toward each other.
2. **Choose the size listed** under each prompt (portrait, landscape or square).
3. **Read every piece of text in the image.** If a word comes out wrong, reply with the matching fix line under that prompt. ChatGPT edits the same image instead of starting over.
4. **Crop to the slot size** and place it in the ad.

## The recurring woman

The same woman appears in every image, so the campaign feels like one story: **late 30s, shoulder-length auburn bob, light freckles, small gold hoop earrings**. She is deliberately unlike Lacey (who has long, dark, wavy hair), so no one mistakes her for Lacey. If one image changes her look, reply: *"Keep everything else, but make the woman match this: late 30s, shoulder-length auburn bob, light freckles, small gold hoop earrings."*

## 1A · Kitchen editorial

**Headline it must show:** "Do you feel trapped choosing between your work and your family?"
**Slot:** 410 × 1080 px, a tall narrow strip on the right of the ad.
**ChatGPT size:** Portrait (1024 × 1536).

```
Create a photorealistic vertical photo, shot like a candid editorial lifestyle image.

Scene: a bright, warm-white kitchen at 3:15 in the afternoon. A woman in her late 30s with a shoulder-length auburn bob, light freckles and small gold hoop earrings stands at a white marble kitchen counter. She wears a cream knit sweater. She is mid video call: a laptop sits open on the counter facing her, and she is leaning toward it with a polite work smile, holding one finger up as if to say "one second".

At the same moment, her daughter, about 5 years old, in a terracotta-coloured dress, stands at her hip, tugging the hem of her sweater with one hand and holding a crayon drawing up toward her with the other. The drawing is clearly visible and says "MOM LOOK!" in big, wobbly, child's crayon letters, with a drawing of a house and a sun.

The mother's eyes are on the laptop, but her body is half turned toward her daughter. She is caught between the two, and the tension should be obvious at a glance.

On the laptop screen (visible at an angle) is a video call with two small video tiles and a red button labelled "Leave". A wall clock behind them reads 3:15.

Composition: tall and narrow. The mother's head and the laptop in the upper half, the child and the "MOM LOOK!" drawing in the lower half. Keep both faces and the drawing in the central 60% of the width, because the sides will be cropped.

Light and colour: soft natural daylight from a window on the left. Palette of warm white, cream and sand beige, with terracotta (the dress) and a touch of deep teal (a tea towel on the counter). No logos on the laptop. No other text in the image.
```
**Fix line if needed:** *"Keep everything, but make the drawing clearly say MOM LOOK! in large crayon letters, and make the laptop show a video call with a red Leave button."*

## Before you publish

- **Read every word** in each image. ChatGPT is good with text now, but still misspells sometimes; use the fix line rather than starting over.
- **Check hands and fingers,** especially in 1A, 2E and 3A.
- **Check for brand logos** on laptops and phones (Apple, Gmail and so on). Ask ChatGPT to remove them if any appear.
- **Make sure the woman doesn't look like Lacey.** If the hair comes out long and dark, use the line under "The recurring woman" above.
