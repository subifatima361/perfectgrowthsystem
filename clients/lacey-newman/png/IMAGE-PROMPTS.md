# Lacey Newman ads: AI image prompts for the six image slots

Six ads (1A, 1D, 2B, 2E, 3A, 3D) have a hatched **IMAGE SLOT** in the PNG. Generate the image with the prompt below, crop it to the slot size, and drop it in. The other 12 ads are finished and need no images.

**No people's faces in any prompt.** In a coach's ad, viewers would assume any woman shown is Lacey. The prompts use hands, empty rooms and objects instead. If you want Lacey in 1A, 1D or 2E, use her real high-res brand photos.

**Every prompt has three versions:**
- **Midjourney:** paste as is. The `--ar` sets the shape.
- **ChatGPT (image generation):** paste the sentence version, then pick the size listed.
- **Adobe Firefly:** paste the same sentence version, set the aspect ratio listed, Content type: Photo.

Generate at the largest size the tool offers, then crop to the slot. Keep the subject inside the middle of the slot, because text sits close to the edges.

---

## Shared style (already included in every prompt below)

Editorial lifestyle photography, soft natural window light, warm neutral palette (sand beige, cream, warm white) with small accents of terracotta and deep teal, gentle shadows, shallow depth of field, calm and uncluttered, film-like grain.

## Before you publish

- **Check every image for AI artefacts:** extra fingers in 1A, warped laptop logos, garbled writing in the planner (3A).
- **Remove any visible brand logos** (Apple and similar) from laptops and phones.
- **Match the colour:** if an image comes out cool or blue, warm it up so it sits with the sand and terracotta in the ad.
